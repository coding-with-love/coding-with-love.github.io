Privacy Policy

BlockZone — Puzzle Game
Last updated: March 2026

At Coding with Love, developers of BlockZone — Puzzle Game, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our application on Android devices.

Summary: BlockZone does not collect personal data. All game data (scores, settings, statistics) is stored locally on your device only. The app uses Google AdMob for advertising, Firebase for crash reporting, and Google Play Billing for in-app purchases.

📱Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. BlockZone does not:

  • Collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information
  • Collect location data (GPS or network-based)
  • Access your contacts, camera, microphone, or external storage
  • Transmit game data (scores, settings) to our servers
  • Run any proprietary tracking or analytics service

💾Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored exclusively on your device using Android's Room Database and DataStore — it never leaves your device:

  • Scores: best score per game mode, recent match history
  • Statistics: total games played, total lines cleared, daily play streak
  • Preferences: selected theme (light/dark/auto), vibration and sound settings, grid size, selected block skin
  • Ad-free status: timestamp of when you earned a rewarded 7-day banner-free period
  • Purchase status: whether you have purchased permanent ad-free or skin packs (verified locally)

All this data:

  • Stays entirely on your device
  • Is inaccessible to other applications
  • Is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app

📢Advertising — Google AdMob

BlockZone displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Advertising ID of the device
  • General device information (model, OS version)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • IP address (for general geographic targeting)

This data is collected directly by Google, not by us. You can review how Google handles this information in the Google Privacy Policy.

🎬Rewarded Ads — Banner-Free Feature

BlockZone offers the option to watch one rewarded video ad to remove banner ads for 7 days, at no cost. When you earn this reward:

  • A timestamp is saved in your device's local storage
  • Banner ads are hidden until 7 days have elapsed from that timestamp
  • No personal data is sent to our servers as a result of this action
  • You can renew the 7-day period before it expires

This rewarded ad is also served by Google AdMob and is subject to the same data practices described above.

🚫Ad Consent — GDPR / CCPA

BlockZone uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect your consent for personalized advertising where required by applicable law (including the EU's GDPR and California's CCPA). You may be shown a consent dialog when you first launch the app. Your consent choice is stored locally and can be changed at any time via the app's settings.

🔥Analytics & Crash Reporting — Firebase

BlockZone uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (both by Google) to improve the app experience:

  • Firebase Analytics: collects aggregated, anonymized usage data (e.g., game mode popularity, feature usage). No personally identifiable information is linked to these events.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: automatically reports app crashes and errors, including device information (model, OS version, app version) and a stack trace. This data helps us identify and fix bugs quickly.

You can review Firebase's data practices at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

💳In-App Purchases

BlockZone offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google — we never receive, store, or process your payment or credit card information.

🔑App Permissions

BlockZone requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET: required to load and display ads, send crash reports, and validate purchases
  • VIBRATE: provides haptic feedback when placing blocks and clearing lines (can be disabled in Settings)
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: required to process in-app purchases through Google Play

We do not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone, external storage, or any other sensitive system permission.

🔒Data Security

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data stored on our servers to protect. The game data stored locally on your device is kept within Android's application sandbox, protected by Android's built-in security model and inaccessible to other apps.

👶Children's Privacy

BlockZone is a casual puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). The app complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) by not collecting any personal data from any user. Ads displayed in the app comply with Google's family content policies.

🔗Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy (AdMob, Firebase, Play Billing, Play Review)
  • Firebase Privacy & Security

🔄Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
